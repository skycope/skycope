# First-visit audit (14 Nov 2026, branch `magic-round2`)

A designer-researcher played the site cold, as a visitor with no context, before reading any doc or source; then read `HANDOVER.md`, `docs/handover/object-audit.md`, the memory notes and the source, and played again with knowledge to confirm or clear each suspicion. No source file was edited. Captures are in `docs/design/first-visit/` (single frames) and `docs/design/first-visit/sheets/` (2x3 contact sheets, row-major, one tile every 1.5-6 s).

How to read the evidence:

- **Cold session**: plain URL, no flags, real key and mouse events over CDP, about 15 minutes of site time. The seed is random per load, and another agent's edit made Vite reload the page once mid-session, so the cold session spans two islands (seeds 4244891173 and 2375752577). There is no `window.skycope` without `?perf`, so cold captures carry no coordinates.
- **Informed session**: `?perf&seed=1847` (the audit's seed), teleporting to approach points and walking in. All coordinates below are seed 1847 `(x, z)` unless marked.
- Nobody heard anything: Chrome ran muted. Everything about sound is in `sound-plan.md`.

The owner's words this is measured against: "paring back on confusing elements" · "many of the new things seemed overwhelming / too much" · "the point isn't 'overwhelm the user with random new glitchy elements' it's 'make the world way more fun and magical to interact with'" · "some of them say things but nothing really happens" · "no real reason to explore right?"

## 3. The first 60 seconds, without text

What a visitor should understand, in order:

1. **I am the cat, and it moves when I do.** (Works today.)
2. **The small light is alive and wants me to come.** (Fails today: it reads as an effect.)
3. **The world answers what I do.** (Fails today: nothing answers in the first ten minutes; text claims things instead.)
4. **There is somewhere to go.** (Fails today: no landmark is in view from the home beach, and the first straight walk ends pinned on a trunk.)

The smallest set of changes that gets there:

- **a. Fix the pins first.** A head-on trunk, the wreck keel and a boulder flank each held the cat for 16-60 s with W down (section 4, G1-G3). Nothing else matters if the first walk ends stuck.
- **b. Make the wisp small, warm and well-mannered** (table no. 1 and no. 13). It appears from behind the nearest rock at cat height, about the size of the cat's eye-shine, waits 5 m off in the open, and moves on only when the cat comes or looks.
- **c. Give the meow an answer in the first 20 s.** The wisp already chimes back (`journey.js`, "wisp" call). Show the meow lesson when the wisp first comes close (no. 8), and make the reply a small bob and a soft warm pulse, not a flare.
- **d. Put one warm light in the first frame at dusk and night**: the boat's post lantern or the lighthouse beam must be visible from the spawn view, or turn the spawn heading until one is. By day, the equivalent is the lighthouse's white tower on the skyline. One landmark in the opening shot is the reason to walk.
- **e. Let the first "found" be earned.** The tortoise is 6.5 m from the spawn. Put it on open sand in view (the audit says the same), drop its radius line, and let the first line be "found · a tortoise" when the cat has sat beside it and the head has come out. That is the site's whole idea in one small act, inside a minute.
- **f. Say less.** No "found" line before that one; no header dots; the photo hint not before the second discovery.

With a-f the first minute reads: a cat on a beach at the real time of day; a small warm light comes out and waits; I walk; I press the key the hint offers and the light answers; there is a shell on the sand that turns out to be alive; far off, a lamp.

## 5. Ten more small discoveries

The register that worked in play: warm practical light in a blue night (the post lantern on the sand, cottage windows, the bow lantern, cyan rings round wading paws), and the world answering what the cat does. Each of these uses a mechanic that already exists, has a hint that needs no text, and is quiet. None repeats the object audit's fifteen. Ranked by joy per effort. The hint is part of each design and has to be built with it, unless it is marked "(exists)".
